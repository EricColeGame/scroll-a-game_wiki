## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the fastest way to obtain rare scroll a game items?
The most efficient route is participating in high-tier cooperative dungeons like the Celestial Spire while utilizing active luck consumables. Running in organized squads maximizes completion speed and allows players to share bonus loot chests.

### Should I dismantle or sell duplicate items?
Duplicate items should generally be preserved for the star fusion system. Fusing duplicates unlocks exclusive secondary passives. If an item has already reached maximum star rank, dismantle it for crafting dust rather than selling it for standard gold.

### How do I know if an item scales with my build?
Check the primary scaling stat listed in the item details window. Physical damage builds require strength or agility scaling, while magical scrolls demand intelligence or elemental affinity modifiers. Equipping mismatched items drastically reduces your overall damage output.

### Can low-rarity items compete with high-tier drops?
In early and mid-game stages, a heavily upgraded Rare item (+7 or higher) will often outperform an un-upgraded Legendary item. However, in endgame progression, Legendary and Mythic items surpass lower rarities due to superior base scaling and unique passive effects.
