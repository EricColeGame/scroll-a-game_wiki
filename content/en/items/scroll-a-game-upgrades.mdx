Progress in scrolling-based progression titles revolves around a balance between active input, passive generation, collection capacity, and fortune mechanics. ## Common Mistakes to Avoid When Upgrading

Even experienced players run into bottlenecks by falling into common resource allocation traps. Steering clear of these pitfalls keeps your progress moving forward without unnecessary stalling.

* **Neglecting Utility Over Raw Numbers:** Pouring everything into raw yield while ignoring collection range forces you to waste valuable seconds chasing down loose items manually.
* **Falling for "Noob Trap" Multipliers:** Some early-game upgrades carry high price tags with deceptive percentage descriptions that only apply to base stats rather than total output.
* **Hoarding Currency Unnecessarily:** Keeping massive balances of unspent currency provides zero passive benefit unless you have an unlocked artifact that specifically scales with bank size.
* **Pre-mature Prestige Triggers:** Resetting before reaching clear milestone thresholds resets your progress without affording you impactful permanent perks.
* **Ignoring Idle Enhancements:** Even active players log off eventually; leaving your offline generation un-upgraded sacrifices hours of free compounding progress.
