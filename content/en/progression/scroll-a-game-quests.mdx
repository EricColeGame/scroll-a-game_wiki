## Frequently Asked Questions About Scroll a Game Quests

### What should I do if my scroll count stops registering for quest progress?
If your action counts are not advancing your active objectives, first check whether your current zone matches the quest's location requirements. Many objectives require you to be in a specific realm or have a particular gear set equipped. If conditions are met, community reports suggest restarting your game client to resynchronize your local session with the progression server.

### Do scroll a game quests reset if I fail a milestone timer?
Timed milestone quests typically reset to zero progress for that specific attempt upon expiration, but they do not lock you out permanently. You can generally reattempt the task as soon as your cooldown expires or after resetting your zone instance. Standard daily and weekly tasks do not fail; they simply refresh when the global timer rolls over.

### Is it better to focus on daily quests or milestone achievements first?
For new and intermediate players, completing daily tasks is consistently more rewarding. Dailies provide immediate currencies and consumables that fuel your character's baseline power. Once your daily checklist is finished, use the remainder of your active session to work toward long-term milestone achievements.

### Can passive or auto-scroll mechanics complete quest objectives while offline?
In most modern progression setups, passive scrolling counts toward general distance and threshold goals, provided you have unlocked the appropriate offline automation upgrades. However, specific tasks requiring item collection, interaction with interactive zone objects, or manual precision prompts usually require active gameplay to register.
